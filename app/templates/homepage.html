Why QuickPay

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

⚡

Instant STK Push

Payment requests appear directly on the customer's phone via Safaricom's Daraja API. No redirects, no confusion.

🔐

JWT-Secured Accounts

Every session is protected with JSON Web Tokens. Merchant dashboards and customer data stay private.

📊

Real-time Dashboard

Merchants see every transaction the moment it happens phone number, amount, status, and timestamp.

💳

No Bank Account Needed

QuickPay works entirely over M-Pesa. Any customer with a mobile wallet can pay instantly.

🇲🇿

Built for Mozambique

Designed from the ground up for Mozambican merchants and customers. MZN currency. Local phone formats.

🔄

Automatic Callback Handling

M-Pesa callbacks are received and processed server-side. Transaction status updates without any manual step.
